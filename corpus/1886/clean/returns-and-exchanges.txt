You are eligible for **returns or exchanges within 7 days:**

* **In-store purchases:** from the date of purchase  
* **Online orders:** from the date of order delivery

**All returned items must be unused, unworn, and returned with original tags and labels attached**.

**Proof of purchase is required** **for all returns.**

**Important Note:Returns do not apply to Archive Collection items or any discounted/sale items.**

---

## **How to Request a Return**

### **Online Purchases**

1. **Email us at care@1886fashion.com** within **7 days from the delivery date**.  
2. A pickup will be arranged with an **additional shipping fee** depending on your location.  
   Please note: it may take **up to 15 business days** for your return to reach us.  
3. Once your return is received and approved by our quality department, your refund will be credited back to the **same payment method used for the purchase** (**excluding shipping fees**).  
   Refunds can take **up to 15 business days**, depending on your payment provider.

---

### **In-Store Purchases**

1. Visit your **nearest 1886 store branch** within **7 days from the purchase date**.  
2. Once the returned item passes our **quality check** (unused and unworn), you will be refunded via the **same payment method** used for your purchase.

**Note:** Shipping fees for online orders are non-refundable.

---

## **Exchanges**

If you wish to exchange your order, you may do so **online or in-store**.

### **Online Exchange**

1. **Email us at care@1886fashion.com** within **7 days from the delivery date**.  
2. A pickup will be arranged with an **additional shipping fee** depending on your location.  
   Please note: it may take **up to 15 business days** for your return to reach us.  
3. Once your return is received and approved by our quality department, your **replacement order will be shipped in a new order.**

 

### **In-Store Exchange**

1. Visit your nearest 1886 store branch within 7 days from the date of purchase.  
2. Exchanges are subject to stock availability and quality inspection.  
* You will either:  
  * Pay the price difference, or  
  * Receive a partial refund  
    based on the value of the exchanged item, using the **original payment method**.

**Note: For orders outside of KSA, Once your returned order is accepted and refunded, we will process your new requested placed order.**  
---

## **Store-Specific Notes**

* Free exchanges and returns are available **only through 1886 store branches**.  
* This policy applies **only to official 1886 stores** and does not include other points of sale.

---

## **Items Not Eligible for Return or Exchange**

* Archive items (Exchange Only)  
* Sale or discounted items (Exchange Only)  
* Custom orders  
* Accessories (bottles, watches, socks.)  
* Underwear and swimwear  
* Gift Cards

---

## **Damaged, Incorrect, or Missing Items**

If you receive an item that is **defective, incorrect, or missing**, please contact us within **3 days of delivery** on **care@1886fashion.com** and include:

* Your order number  
* Clear photos of the item(s)  
* Any relevant details

---

## **Additional Information**

* All items must be returned with the **original packaging protected by an outer layer**.  
* Refused deliveries will be sent back to sender and **shipping costs will apply**.  
* **Proof of purchase is required** for all returns.  
* Do not send returns to the manufacturer.
